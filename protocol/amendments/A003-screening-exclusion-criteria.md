## Does not change

- research questions;
- search strings;
- information sources;
- review interval;
- Layer 1 scope;
- Layer 2 analytical purpose;
- manual collection status.

## Rationale

- reduce ambiguity during screening;
- avoid excessive branching of exclusion rules;
- preserve Layer 1 mapping studies even when they do not provide extractable quality evidence;
- support later analysis of syntactic and semantic quality evidence without making quality evaluation a Layer 1 exclusion criterion.

## Decision

The protocol exclusion criteria are consolidated into ten operational rules. When more than one rule applies, the first criterion that clearly explains the exclusion is recorded. Ambiguous title and abstract cases are retained for full-text screening.

Layer 1 eligibility remains independent of the availability of extractable quality evidence. Reports within the Layer 1 scope that do not provide such evidence remain in the mapping corpus and are marked as not eligible for Layer 2 synthesis. Three extraction fields record the availability of syntactic evidence, semantic evidence, and evidence of syntax-semantic dissonance.
